FitRival privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-05-16

FitRival ("the app") is published by Sage Arbor (the "Developer"). This policy describes what data the app collects, where it goes, and how to delete it.

Data collected

The app collects the following data only when you actively enter it:

TypeExamplesStored where
Body measurements weight (kg), body-fat %, date, notes On your device (Hive) + the developer's Google Sheet only if you enable cloud sync for a group
Goals target weight, target body-fat %, target date Same as above
Friend records names + optional email + optional phone On your device only — friend records are not uploaded
Workouts exercise names, set reps/weights, durations, notes On your device only
Apple Health / Health Connect imports weight + body-fat % imported with explicit OS permission Same path as manual entries above

The app does not collect: IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising IDs, location, contacts in bulk, photos, or any analytics events.

Cloud sync (opt-in, per-group)

When you press "Enable cloud sync" on a group (or paste a friend's join code), that group's body measurements and goals are written to a Google Sheet owned by the Developer. Rows for different groups are isolated by per-group capability tokens — a secret paired with each group's ID. The Sheet's contents are not visible to anyone who does not possess a given group's join code.

You can disable cloud sync for a group at any time via the "Unlink" action. Disabling stops further pushes/pulls but does not delete the rows already in the Sheet — see Deletion below.

Sharing

The Developer does not sell or share your data with third parties. The Google Sheet that holds cloud-synced data is hosted on Google Drive under the Developer's Google account; access is restricted to the Developer and to anyone who holds your group's join code.

Deletion

  • On-device data: uninstall the app from your phone or desktop. All Hive data is removed with the app.
  • Cloud-synced data: email sophie.arborbot@gmail.com with the join code(s) of the group(s) you want to remove. The Developer will delete the matching rows within 14 days.

Children

FitRival is not intended for children under 13. Do not use it if you are under 13.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above and noted in the App Store / Play Store changelog.
