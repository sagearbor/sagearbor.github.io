Mosaic HighRes privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Mosaic HighRes ("the app") is published by Sage Arbor (the "Developer"). This policy describes what the app does with your data — in short: everything happens on your device, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

There is no server. Mosaic HighRes has no backend, no account system, and no analytics. The app was built this way on purpose and that is not going to change.

Data the app accesses

WhatWhyLeaves the device?
Your photo library (read access) To let you pick a "target" photo and a pool of photos used as mosaic tiles Never
The mosaic image the app builds The whole point of the app — a new image file assembled from tiles of your pool photos Never, unless you explicitly tap Share and pick a destination (e.g. Messages, email, Google Photos) — that is the normal Android/iOS share sheet handing the file to an app of your choice, not Mosaic HighRes uploading it anywhere itself

On Android, the app requests the standard photo-library permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, or the Android 14+ "select photos" flow) and nothing broader. On iOS it requests Photos access through the standard system picker.

What the app does NOT do

  • No account, no sign-up, no login — there is nothing to create and nothing to log into.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting that leaves the device, no advertising SDKs, no trackers of any kind.
  • No photo, thumbnail, or rendered mosaic is ever uploaded, synced, or transmitted over the network by the app.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data are collected.

The one network request the app can make

The Support screen has an optional "Tip $5 on Venmo" button. Tapping it opens Venmo (or your browser, if Venmo isn't installed) to the Developer's public tip page — this is the only circumstance under which the app causes any network request, and it only happens if you deliberately tap that button. The rest of the app — picking photos, building the mosaic, saving or sharing the result — works identically with no network connection at all.

Where exported files go

When you tap "Save to Photos," the finished mosaic is written to your device's own photo library, using the standard OS save APIs — the same place any photo you take goes. When you tap "Share," the operating system's normal share sheet opens and the file goes wherever you pick (a message, an email, another app). The Developer has no visibility into, and no copy of, anything you export.

Data retention and deletion

Nothing is stored anywhere except on your own device, so there is nothing for the Developer to retain or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app itself created (its project data, cached thumbnails, and any exports saved inside the app's own storage). Exports you explicitly saved to your photo library or shared elsewhere are yours to manage like any other file — the app has no way to reach back and delete them, and does not try to.

Children

Mosaic HighRes is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, child or adult — see above.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above and noted in the Play Store listing's changelog.
